        Environments

        Group the origins of one app once. Then Ctrl+Alt+E (or Ctrl+Shift+E) cycles local → staging → production, keeping path, query and hash. Leave an origin empty to skip it.

        Command bar

        Must start with http(s):// and contain %s for the query. Search text is only sent when you press Enter on a search result.

        Camera, microphone and screen sharing

        Sites ask before using your camera, microphone or screen; Quiver shows the question in the toolbar. Known meeting sites (Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Jitsi, Whereby) can ask; other sites are denied until they ask and you agree. Choices you saved:

          Privacy

          Quiver sends no telemetry, has no account and does not check for updates. Crash reports stay on this device. These settings apply to all Spaces.

          Tell sites not to sell or share your data (Sec-GPC and DNT headers).
          Keep WebRTC calls from revealing addresses on your local network.
          Uses the Windows spell checker; nothing is downloaded.
          Delete cookies, site storage and cache in every Space when Quiver closes. You will be signed out of sites; tabs, bookmarks and notes are kept.

          Downloads

          Show Save As for every download. When off, files go straight to your Downloads folder (the Data folder in Quiver Portable) and get a number if the name is taken.

          Tabs

          Tabs below the divider that you haven't looked at for this long move to the archive. Pinned tabs, the tab you're on in each Space and tabs playing sound stay. Type archive in the command bar to search and reopen them. Closing a pinned tab takes it back to its pinned page instead of removing it.

          Local servers

          Lists HTTP servers listening on this machine (netstat + a local request for the page title). Pauses while the window is in the background.
